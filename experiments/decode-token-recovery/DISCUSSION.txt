INTERPRETATION FOR THE DECODE EXTENSION

Direct features support generated-token classification on both models. With
32-column groups, Top-1 is 74.93% for Qwen and 65.89% for Llama on 1536 test tokens
each; balanced accuracy is 42.41% and 35.15%. These scores exceed the frequency,
position, and permuted-feature baselines. No full 32-token test prefix is exact.

Real cache recovery depends strongly on the measurement regime. With 25 gathers
(stride5 x5 repeats), filtered decode precision/recall is 98.63/94.00% for AMD
Qwen and 91.42/93.59% for AMD Llama, versus 60.07/55.62% and 69.54/66.18% on Intel.
One-gather full scans are much noisier. Changing 1/5 to 25 gathers changes both
the scan schedule and replay budget; this is not a repeat-count-only ablation.

The paired classifier bridge uses 128 token positions per model/platform and
the frozen direct-trained group32 classifier, without cache-specific retraining.
Filtered 25-gather Top-1 losses relative to direct features on the SAME cohort
are 0.78/1.56 percentage points on AMD and 3.12/1.56 on Intel (Qwen/Llama).
Response-bootstrap intervals are in DIAGNOSTICS.txt and results.json. These
small cohorts do not establish a universal within 1-percentage-point guarantee.
No measured eight-token response prefix is completely recovered by the bridge.

High false-positive rates also occur with gather disabled. Fresh flush/reload
per target reduces them to 0-0.04%, while full scans remain much noisier. This
supports measurement-induced contamination; it does not identify a specific
hardware prefetcher. Fixed four-column controls distinguish input shape effects
from changing live activation footprints. Real prefill footprints are dense
under this P99.9 interpretation, and consecutive-group filtering suppresses
many true groups. New prefill results do not reproduce the paper's sparse,
high-recall setting and should not be silently substituted into its old table.

Cross-platform comparisons use identical checkpoints, inputs, thresholds, and
probe code, but FP16 CPU arithmetic is not bit-identical. All 144 generated IDs
match for Qwen; 135/144 match for Llama, including the unconsumed next outputs.
Live-mask differences are quantified in verification.json. Synthetic fixed-
column controls provide the comparison with exactly identical ground truth.

Publication scope: add a separately labeled reconstructed-P99.9 decode study,
with direct classification, real fidelity, and paired classification distinguished.
Disclose in-process synchronization and replay. Do not claim an unmodified,
independent victim attack, NPU acceleration, complete response recovery, or
exact reproduction of unspecified original top-k/robust-z settings. The current
Overleaf manuscript and GitHub repository have not been modified in this task.
